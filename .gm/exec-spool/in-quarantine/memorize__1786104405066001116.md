## Resolved mutable: numerical-stack-choice

Research confirms candle (huggingface/candle) compiles to wasm32-unknown-unknown with proven browser demos (candle-wasm-examples: Whisper, SAM, LLaMA2, Yolo). candle-nn::Conv1dConfig natively supports padding/stride/dilation/groups (covers dilated+depthwise convs needed for WaveNet/AMPBlock); ConvTranspose1d and GroupNorm exist as documented structs; SnakeBeta trivially expressible via Tensor sin/powf/broadcast ops. No built-in unfold/im2col in either candle or ndarray -- location-variable convolution requires a handwritten gather regardless of stack, so this is not a differentiator. Known WASM gotcha: native-dep features (libloading) must be disabled for wasm32 build (github.com/huggingface/candle issue #2736, fixed PR #2760). Perf precedent: candle-wasm Whisper tiny/base runs ~2-3x realtime in-browser, single-threaded (no WASM threads/SIMD parallelism wired up in published examples). Decision: use candle + candle-nn as the numerical stack, hand-write only the LVC unfold/gather op and the FIR/resample math (rustfft or candle FFT as needed) on top of it.
